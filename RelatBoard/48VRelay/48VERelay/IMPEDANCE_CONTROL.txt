CONTROLLED IMPEDANCE REQUIREMENTS

Board:
- 4 layers
- Finished thickness: 1.6 mm
- Outer copper: 1 oz
- Inner copper: 0.5 oz
- Use manufacturer-approved controlled-impedance stackup

Ethernet MDI pairs:
- Nets: TX_P / TX_N
- Nets: RX_P / RX_N
- Routing layer: L1 / Top Copper
- Reference plane: L2 / continuous GND
- Target differential impedance: 100 ohms
- Tolerance: +/-10%

Designed geometry:
- Trace width: 8.89mil
- Pair spacing, edge-to-edge: 8mil
- Copper-to-nearby-copper clearance: 8mil

The manufacturer may adjust trace width and/or spacing as required
to achieve 100-ohm differential impedance, but must notify the
customer before making changes that violate clearances or footprint
breakouts.
